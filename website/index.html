The loop

Three moves, one club

Geo-Kick turns a walk to the local pitch into a football manager. The core loop is simple.

Check in on location

Find a real football pitch on the map and check in with GPS. Five minutes earns coins; a full session guarantees a pack — with anti-cheat so only real visits count.

Open packs, collect players

Every pack is a full-screen reveal from weakest to best. Chase Gold and Legendary cards — or the one-off ??? 99-rated mystery player.

Build your XI, climb the league

Set your formation and best eleven, then rise through four divisions of live-ticker matches — promotions, trophies and a season of your own.

In the game

A whole season in your pocket

Far more than a card collector — a full matchday engine you actually play.

Live-ticker matches

Minute-by-minute commentary, scorers, yellow and red cards, xG, possession and a Man of the Match rated out of 10.

Tournaments & pitch battles

Play a Champions-League-style cup and challenge boss teams that only appear at real pitches — decided by interactive penalty shootouts.

Online friendlies

Challenge a friend live: both sides watch the exact same match, make their own halftime changes, and settle draws player-versus-player.

Transfer market & season pass

A fresh AI market every day with lightning deals, plus a weekly pass of missions and rewards to keep your squad growing.

Real GPS, real anti-cheat

Geofencing, mock-location detection and a motion check keep it honest. Even the eggs you collect hatch by walking real distance.

Collect & upgrade

Hundreds of players across five rarities, team chemistry by position, level-ups and a captain system with goal and assist bonuses.
