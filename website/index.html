Geo-Kick

A football game you play in real life.

Visit real football pitches, collect players, and build your own club.

Download for Android For newer phones (64-bit) For older phones 32-bit version

When you open the file, Android may ask you to allow the install. Tap Allow, then Install.

Free. Works offline. No real money.

How it works

01

Go to a real pitch

Check in at a football pitch with GPS. You get coins and a pack of players.

02

Open packs

Collect players from Bronze up to Legendary, and one secret star.

03

Build your club

Pick your best team and win your way from Division 4 to Division 1.

Also in the game

  • Live matches with real stats
  • Cup tournaments and pitch battles
  • Play against friends online
  • A new transfer market every day
  • Real GPS with anti-cheat
  • Collect and level up players
Made as a personal project. Code on GitHub.
